What we work on

Research

Six areas of expertise that together cover an industrial plant from end to end.

Industrial process sustainability

Waste to Wealth

Turning biomass, sludge and industrial waste into fuels, activated carbon and chemicals.

  • Pyrolysis, gasification and combustion
  • Activated carbon from biomass
  • Catalytic conversion of coal, biomass and gas

Recent funded projects

  • Preparation of Materials for Commercial-Scale Composting with Biochar (2024)
  • Adsorption of Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) using Newly Improved Hybrid Carbon Gel Derived from Coconut Shell: Synthesis, Characterization and Optimization (2023)
  • Composting with Biochar: Process Optimization for Commercialization (2023)
  • Formulation and Characterization of Cosmecuetical Products Enhanced with Activated Carbon from Bamboo (dendrocalamus Asper) and its by Product (vinegar) (2022)

Process systems

Separation & Membranes

Nanofibre membranes, photocatalysis and enzyme membrane reactors for cleaner separations and produced-water treatment.

  • Electrospun nanofibre membranes
  • Photocatalytic water treatment
  • Enzyme membrane reactors

Recent funded projects

  • Development of SCOBY Membrane as Living Filtration for Sustainable Water Disinfection (2025)
  • Effective VOC Removal from Wastewater Effluent using ZSM-5 Pervaporation Membrane (2024)
  • Synergistic Effects of Band Gap Narrowing Through Ti3+ Self-Doping and Oxygen Vacancies in Black TiO2 as a Promising Homojunction Photocatalyst for Visible Light-Driven CO2 Reduction (2024)
  • Suppression of the coffee-ring effect in membrane-protein dot blotting by adapting solute addition (2023)

Industrial process reliability

Process Safety & Risk

CIMAH safety reports, quantitative risk assessment, facility siting and inherent safety for major hazard installations.

  • CIMAH safety reports
  • Quantitative risk assessment and consequence modelling
  • Hazardous area classification and facility siting

Recent funded projects

  • Groundwater Quality and Potential Health Risk Assessment in Sarawak (2024)
  • Conceptual Dynamic Reliability and Risk Assessment Framework in the Control of Major Accident Hazard using Bayesian Approach (2021)
  • Dynamic Risk Assessment Method Coupled with Material Strength and Consequence Real Time Analysis in Process Safety Optimization (2020)
  • Quantitative Risk Reliability Study on the Design High Pressurized Strength Reactor (400 to 900 bar) for Green Methanol Synthesis from Carbon Dioxide (CO2) Hydrogenation process route (2020)

Environmental engineering

Pollution Control

Adsorbents, air-pollution control and wastewater treatment design that keep plants within environmental limits.

  • Adsorbents and CO₂ capture
  • Wastewater treatment design
  • Air pollution control and EIA

Recent funded projects

  • Equilibrium and Kinetic Studies in Adsorption of Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) using Coconut Shell Activated Carbon Xerogel (CSACX) (2025)
  • Development Of VCI And CCI Formulation For The Carbon Dioxide (Sweet) And Hydrogen Sulfide (Sour) Environments In The Oil And Gas Industry With The Objective Of Commercializing The Said Formulation (2024)
  • Pb(II) adsorption mechanism and capability from aqueous solution using Leucaena leucocephala bark modified by NaOH (2023)
  • Heterogenous Fenton Chemistry: Mechanisms of Degradation Kinetics of Plastic Pollutants by the Interaction Between Iron Bearing Soil Minerals and Glutathione in Soil and Groundwater (2022)

Process systems engineering

Process Design & Optimisation

Designing, operating and optimising plants for safety, energy and cost, from lab scale to process validation.

  • Process design and simulation
  • Lab-to-plant process validation
  • Catalysis, energy and fuel systems

Recent funded projects

  • Dynamic Human Vulnerability Accident Risk Modelling using Chemical Process Historical Database and Bayesian Tree Augmented Naïve Algorithm on Crude Oil Distillation (2023)
  • Evaluation of Activation Energy of Ni supported MgAl2O4 and unsupported Ni catalyst and its correlation with active metal sites and catalytic activity of dry reforming of methane (2022)
  • Flame Integral Model Based Geometrical Parameters on Buoyancy/Momentum-Controlled Jet Diffusion Flame (2020)
  • Role of Tungsten Carbide (WC) as a promoter on Ni/Al2MgO4 Catalyst for Dry Methane Reforming (DMR) (2020)

Industrial process reliability

Corrosion Engineering

Keeping oil and gas pipelines running: sweet and sour corrosion, top-of-line corrosion, inhibitors and coupon monitoring, with UiTM’s Industrial Corrosion Research Centre (InCORE).

  • Top-of-line corrosion in wet gas pipelines
  • Corrosion inhibitors
  • Coupon analysis for refineries and petrochemical plants

Recent funded projects

  • Insight into Dynamic Redox Transformation of Synergistic H2S/CO2 Corrosion Evolution of Carbon Steel in Mitigating the Risk using Hybrid Intelligent Algorithm Prediction Models (2024)
  • Investigation on Corrosion Inhibition Mechanism Through Diffusion and Adsorption of Thiols in Co-Condensation of Water/N-Heptane in Top-Of-Line Corrosion (TLC) Environment. (2021)
  • Corrosion Inhibition Mechanism of Thiols Against Formation of Iron (II) Sulphide (FES) Polymorph in Marginal Sour Top of Line Corrosion (TLC) Environment (2021)
  • Investigation on Corrosion Inhibition Mechanism Through Diffusion and Adsorption of Thiols in Co-Condensation of Water/N-Heptane in Top-Of-Line Corrosion (TLC) Environment (2020)
