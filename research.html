A-01

Corrosion engineering

Wet gas pipelines corrode from the top as well as the bottom, where water condenses on cold steel. Dr Najmiddin's team studies sweet and sour corrosion, tests inhibitors that travel with the vapour, and reads coupons pulled from live lines at Pengerang and from PTTEP flexible pipes.

  1. 1Top-of-line corrosion in sweet and sour wet gas
  2. 2Volatile corrosion inhibitors
  3. 3Coupon analysis and interpretation for refineries and petrochemical plants

A-02

Process safety and risk

If a site stores enough chlorine, LPG, ethylene oxide or methanol, Malaysian law requires a CIMAH safety report. Dr Azil and Prof. Zulkifli have written them for chemical plants, glove factories, semiconductor fabs, ports and PETRONAS fuel terminals since 2016.

A-03

Separation and membranes

Spinning polymer into fibres a fraction of a hair wide gives a membrane that can strip oil out of produced water. Dr Nur Hashimah works on those membranes and on light-driven catalysts. Dr Fauziah puts enzymes inside membrane reactors so the reaction and the separation happen in one step.

  2. 2Photocatalytic water treatment

A-04

Waste to wealth

Malaysia produces mountains of palm fibre, coconut shell and sewage sludge. We heat them, with or without air, into biochar, activated carbon, hydrochar, bio-oil and hydrogen. Then we test whether anyone would pay for the result.

Fig. A-04.1 · line art, not to scale

  1. 01 / 04

    Feedstock

    Palm empty fruit bunch, coconut shell, landscape waste, sewage sludge.

  2. 02 / 04

    Heat, little or no air

    Pyrolysis, carbonisation or hydrothermal treatment. The first trial ran in a steel oil drum.

  3. 03 / 04

    Char

    Biochar, hydrochar, activated carbon after steam activation; bio-oil and hydrogen as other products.

  4. 04 / 04

    Use

    Adsorbent for H2S and effluent, compost and slow-release fertiliser, solid fuel.

  1. 1Pyrolysis, carbonisation and hydrothermal treatment
  2. 2Biochar and activated carbon from biomass
  3. 3Catalytic conversion of coal, biomass and gas
