What we do

From waste heap to control room

Every unit in a process plant is a place where things can go wrong, or be done better. Tap any part of the plant to see how INPRES works on it.

Hover or tap a unit. Gold-and-purple dashes show the process flow; the magnifier shows pitting corrosion on a buried pipeline.

S

Pillar one

Process sustainability

Environmental engineering and process systems engineering: making plants cleaner and wasting less.

  • Waste to wealth, biomass and energy
  • Pollution control and adsorption
  • Process design, operation and optimisation
R

Pillar two

Process reliability

Process safety, risk and corrosion engineering: keeping hazardous plants and pipelines running safely.

  • CIMAH safety reports and quantitative risk analysis
  • Facility siting, inherent safety, hazardous area classification
  • Oil and gas pipeline and top-of-line corrosion
