Capability statement

INPRES

Industrial Process Reliability & Sustainability

Research group, Faculty of Chemical Engineering, Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM), Shah Alam, Malaysia. UiTM Entiti Kecemerlangan Tier 4, CoRe1/T4/2019(1)/FMIA(1).

01Who we are

Seven chemical engineers, all registered Professional Engineers with the Board of Engineers Malaysia; four are ASEAN Chartered Professional Engineers, and Prof. Zulkifli is a DOSH-registered CIMAH competent person. UiTM Centre of Excellence (Tier 4) since 2019. Head: Assoc. Prof. Ir. Dr. Azil Bahari Alias, Dean of the Faculty of Chemical Engineering.

02Services

  • CIMAH safety reports; toxic, flammable and explosion assessments
  • Quantitative risk assessment, consequence modelling, facility siting, inherent safety
  • Hazardous area classification, including combustible dust
  • Corrosion coupon analysis and interpretation; inhibitor efficiency testing; top-of-line corrosion studies
  • Material qualification testing for transmission equipment
  • Lab-scale process validation and optimisation, with mass balance and P&ID
  • Wastewater, air pollution control and adsorbent development
  • Biochar, activated carbon and waste-to-energy studies
  • Industry training: CIMAH, quantitative risk assessment, pipeline corrosion in CO2 and H2S, plant operator programmes
