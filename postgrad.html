V-301

  1. Industry-linked topics

    Plenty of our theses begin as a client’s question: a corrosion coupon from PETRONAS, an earthing problem from TNB, a sludge nobody wants.

  2. Supervised by PEs

    Every supervisor is a registered Professional Engineer with live research grants.

  3. Publish, then travel

    Students publish in indexed journals and work with partners in Australia, the UK and Saudi Arabia.

How to apply

  1. Pick a supervisor from the seven above, by the problem you want to work on.
  2. Email them first. Send your CV and one page on what you want to find out.
  3. Apply through IPSis, UiTM’s Institute of Postgraduate Studies, once a supervisor has agreed to talk.
